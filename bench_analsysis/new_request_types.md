# Newly surfaced request and expected-result distinctions

All **280 questions across the 20 selected buildings** use the documented **nine scored kinds and six warm-up kinds**. No new official `kind` was found. The discoveries below are underdocumented subtypes, eligibility rules and source ambiguities within those kinds. They must not be presented as an announced Sunday schema or hidden-test contract.

| Observed distinction | Source evidence and expected result | Implementation consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Area requests can select hall spaces | `proc-factory-dev-005-H02`: six warehouse halls total **1522.5 m²**. `osm-way27917482-H01`: largest lobby is **Rechnerhalle, 298.10 m²**, a `type=hall` space. | Filter exact requested `room_type`; do not globally require `type=room` for area queries. |
| Shared-wall “rooms” include factory bays | `proc-factory-dev-003-H05`: **7 neighbours = five rooms + two hall bays**. `proc-factory-dev-011-H05`: **4 = two rooms + two halls**. | Shared-wall eligibility differs from delivery-count eligibility. Geometry must also exclude circulation/outdoor spaces. |
| Delivery counts exclude reachable halls | `proc-factory-dev-007-H03`: **8 rooms**, despite eight additional reachable hall spaces. | Count target-floor `type=room` spaces, not every reachable graph node or every non-corridor. |
| The already-entered start room counts | `resplan-8269-H01`: **4 rooms**, including Living room 0.01. `resplan-14509-H01`: **3**, also including start. | Reachability counts include an eligible starting room. All selected entrance doors admit the load; narrow/blocked-entry semantics need separate testing. |
| Width equality matters; premature rounding changes results | `proc-factory-dev-003-H03`: **7 upstairs rooms** through lift doors exactly **1.1 m** wide. `resplan-15179-H02`: **6 rooms**; **1.096 m** openings fail a **1.1 m** load. | Compare unrounded widths with `>=`; filter lift access openings as well as ordinary doors. Connector objects supply no cabin width to invent. |
| Nearest pickup is chosen before delivery optimization | `swiss-460-H03`: **15.72 m**, even though another pickup gives **14.26 m** total. `proc-factory-dev-003-H04`: **132.35 m**, rather than the global two-leg optimum **108.91 m**. | Minimize start→pickup first; then route to the largest requested destination. An unordered/best-tour optimizer solves a different problem. |
| Outdoor pickup locations remain valid | `resplan-1677-H03`: pick up the plant on **Balcony 0.09**, total **19.82 m**. `swiss-5097-H03`: pickup **Balcony 2.61**, total **31.15 m**. | Asset pickup candidates include outdoor spaces even though counted-room queries exclude them. Deduplicate assets by containing space. |
| A nonzero outage count survives a lift closure | `osm-way22744230-H03`: close **Lift 1 / V07**, still **30 rooms on floor 2** reachable step-free from the stated stairwell entrance. | Use graph reachability after the actual closure; neither a stairwell name nor one failed lift establishes inaccessibility. |
| Same-floor endpoints can be inaccessible or need a floor detour | `swiss-5097-Q06`: **unreachable step-free on floor 2**. `swiss-1689-Q07`: **156.85 m** ordered tour, with a same-floor first leg that descends to ground. | Preserve constraints and computed floor sequence. Same-floor requests cannot be answered by Euclidean proximity or assumed monotone floor travel. |
| Floor-qualified text can still be ambiguous | `osm-way27917482-Q09` names **Generic room 03.08.033, Floor 3**; **three spaces** share that name/ref/floor. The label selects `F3-S016`, **154.82 m** after closure; alternatives give **156.65/153.12 m**. | Ask for a distinguishing location/selection. A labelled-ID oracle bypasses this ambiguity and cannot establish one-turn conversational accuracy. Current resolution safely rejects the ambiguous name. |
| What-if doors can join two corridors and affect a different floor downward | `osm-way27917482-H08`: floor-2 corridor door improves **6 floor-3 rooms**. `-H09`: ground-floor room door improves **2 basement rooms**. | Simulate in isolated state; compare every eligible room on the requested floor with a strict **>1 m** improvement. Do not permanently apply the hypothetical edit. |
| A correct aggregate can conceal incorrect door geometry | H08's shared segment midpoint is **(43.274, -20.3995)**; current `add_door` chooses endpoint **(42.881, -21.798)**. Its ad-hoc counts happen to match **6/2** for H08/H09. H09's exact boundary intersection is a `MultiPoint`. | Assert contact/midpoint geometry and state immutability separately from the final count. Exact polygon-boundary overlap alone is insufficient. |
| Local routes explicitly use outside/re-entry | `osm-way27917482-Q05`: ground→floor 3 in **51.77 m** through `outside`. Swiss warm-up paths also leave/re-enter. | Preserve the local anchor-model shortcut and depict discontinuities honestly. These warm-ups do not establish whether scored questions benefit from the shortcut or satisfy Synera physical routing. |
| Floor numbers, elevations and clear heights differ | `osm-way27326387` has levels **-2, 0, 1**; its H08 3D link spans elevations **-6 and +3 m** and expects **9.30 m**. Factory ground clear height is **8 m**, but office elevations are **3.5/7 m**. | Use `elevation_m` for XYZ distances. These stacking labels do not span the missing level; a gap-spanning overlap test is additional proposed coverage. |
| Complete inventories exceed the search display limit | `swiss-5097-Q02`: **19 wardrobes**, 15 displayed. `osm-way27917482-H02`: sum **250 polygons**. `osm-way360544293-H03`: **53 chairs in 22 pickup rooms**, errand **163.96 m**. | Expose exact counts/aggregates and complete candidate sets; do not ask the model to sum a truncated page or route once per asset. |
| Search total is not an exact asset count | `osm-way22744230-Q04`: labelled **3 toilet assets**; current search reports **7 matches**, mixing room names and assets. | Separate entity kind and exact asset type. Other matching sample counts do not prove a generic count tool exists. |
| Route/tour labels require richer provenance than a length | Reachable warm-up labels contain both `space_path` and `element_path`; tours have **two explicit legs**. `proc-factory-dev-003-Q10`: **276.28 m**, legs **150.03/126.25 m**. | Keep actual selected doors/connectors, leg boundaries and outside transitions for validation/rendering. Fixed-order tours do not authorize reordering. |
| Duplicate prompts inflate raw coverage | ResPlan pairs `8269-Q05/Q06`, `15179-Q06/Q07`, `1677-Q05/Q06` repeat prompt and labels. | Count all source rows in the audit; select one member of each identical pair in representative behavioral testing. |

The normalized [subtype table](question_subtypes.md) and [full ledger](questions.csv) attach classifications and source lines to every question. Independent family evidence is in the [OSM](reviews/osm.md), [procedural](reviews/procedural.md) and [residential](reviews/residential.md) reports.

## Requirements without observed examples

No selected file requests forklift-only routing, destination-only production-bay traversal, wall-respecting in-space routes, physical-height route costs, a building constructed from a plan, moving equipment, optimized unordered tours, or a new Sunday file schema. The first four route rules are already documented Synera requirements; plan ingestion belongs to the required product levels. Their absence here does not remove them from the product scope or establish passing coverage.

The 20-building audit does not determine no-overlap/tie behavior for stacked rooms, impossible entry semantics, unreachable→reachable what-if improvement counting, or every geometric threshold boundary. [Proposed regressions](tests.md#additional-regressions-once-the-corresponding-tools-exist) identify these uncertainties explicitly instead of fabricating organizer expectations.
